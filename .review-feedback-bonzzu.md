## Part B — Narrative suggestions

### Missed keywords/requirements

- **"SDK guidance" is an honest gap — leave it visible.** The JD asks for SDK guidance improvement; nothing on file supports SDK authorship or contribution. Do not stuff it. If the candidate has actually used Asana's or Notion's official SDKs (node/python client libraries), that's worth one truthful phrase in the Platform & API competency; ask him. Otherwise the REST/OAuth/webhooks coverage is a credible adjacent.
- **"Office hours" — no claim available.** The honest adjacents (workshops, 1:1 developer support, lightning-talk events) are already in the CV. Don't invent anything closer.
- **"Internal briefings / developer sentiment synthesis"** — the Wiz bullet ("raised it with the team") and IBM Design Thinking bullet already carry this implicitly. If the drafter wants the JD's phrase "structured feedback collection" in the Developer enablement competency, it's defensible via the documented "structured stakeholder feedback loops," but don't use it twice — it's already in Embedded partner delivery.
- **"8 years" in the profile statement is defensible but know the math.** IBM full-time started Aug 2018, so the span is exactly 8 years — but it includes degree-overlap internships and a 2021–2023 gap-with-internships. He can defend it in an interview without backtracking ("8 years since my first full-time engineering role at IBM"), so keep it, but he should have that sentence ready. Also note the IBM tools served internal support engineers, not external developers — "developer-facing" is a slight stretch there; the Notion work carries the true DevRel weight, which is why publishing the content series matters (below).
- **The single highest-leverage move isn't an edit: publish the Notion content before submitting.** The JD demands "examples and technical content that developers actually use," and the quickstart, sample Worker, and data-sources migration breakdown are marked in-progress in the profile. Even one published piece with a linkable URL converts the CV's weakest factual point into its strongest exhibit, and lets you revert the "Ship→Create" edit.

### Company/department-specific angles (verified vs. unverified)

- **Verified — the agentic platform moment is real and current.** Agents API + AI Teammates in early access with a phased rollout, opt-in via the `Asana-Enable: ai_teammate_actors` header (https://forum.asana.com/t/upcoming-ai-teammates-and-agent-api-support/1142767), MCP server v2 at `mcp.asana.com/v2/mcp` (https://developers.asana.com/docs/using-asanas-mcp-server), AI Studio no-code agent workflows built on the Rules framework (https://asana.com/product/ai/ai-studio). The letter currently mentions none of this; edits 10 and 12 fix that.
- **Verified — a live migration-comms need he has literally done before.** Asana's beta v1 MCP endpoint (`mcp.asana.com/sse`) is deprecated with a hard shutdown on 05/11/2026 (same docs page: https://developers.asana.com/docs/using-asanas-mcp-server). His Notion 2025 data-sources migration breakdown is the exact same genre of work: explaining a breaking platform change to external developers. Strong interview talking point; could also replace the generic half of the closing if the drafter prefers it over the Agents API angle.
- **Verified but use with care — the ecosystem needs reviving.** In a public forum thread, an Asana DevRel team member acknowledged App Components had seen little investment and that they're "looking into ways to re-activate investments" (https://forum.asana.com/t/are-app-components-getting-any-investment/527898). This is almost certainly *why this role exists*. Do not cite the thread in the letter (it reads as criticism); do use it to shape interview positioning: he's walked into under-instrumented systems and rebuilt momentum before.
- **Verified — Bonzzu language mirror.** Bonzzu's site leads with "embedded in your team… always your ally" and "productive from day one" (https://bonzzu.com/); Eric Montoya is a Managing Partner there. The CV's "embedded partner" framing already mirrors this well — keep it. The 30-day close (edit 12) doubles as a "productive from day one" signal to Bonzzu.
- **Verified — Asana culture hooks.** Mission ("help humanity thrive by enabling the world's teams to work together effortlessly") and the "Act Like You Own It" extreme-ownership value are in the FY2026 10-K (https://investors.asana.com/static-files/9b79a79f-ece8-4f9c-93de-e61eb6aee065), along with the "Asana on Asana" power-user culture. Optional: one sentence in the "embedded partner model" paragraph connecting StaffRoom's own stated mission (harmonious human-machine relationships — his authentic words per the behavioral profile) to Asana's mission. His past letters always carry a mission thread and this one currently has none. Only add it if the page budget allows after edits 11–12; the letter must stay at one page.
- **Unverified — do not claim.** No evidence found that Asana currently runs a developer office-hours program; don't reference one as existing. The greeting edit (7) assumes Eric is the right addressee; if the letter will be forwarded directly to Asana stakeholders, "Dear Eric and the Bonzzu team," is the safer variant — drafter's call.

### Action-oriented reframing

- The drafts are largely in good shape here: "Diagnosed," "Built," "Train," "Sell and deliver" are all active, and "I recognized the work immediately" is a strong opener.
- The two weak spots were the closing ("I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how I can be…" — passive, generic, and cliched) and the letter's overall backward-looking inventory structure; edit 12 converts the close into a forward-looking commitment mirroring the JD's own success criteria, which is what 03-writing-style.md asks for ("focus on tasks you can solve for the employer").
- "I would approach that pillar the way I approach any system" is conditional but appropriately so — it's the honest register Eric asked for. Edit 11 grounds it in a past action so it doesn't float.

### Tone and style issues

- **Em-dashes:** none found in either draft — pass (LaTeX `--` range dashes in dates are fine).
- **Cliches:** "force multiplier" was the only real offender (fixed in edit 12). "Familiar ground" is idiomatic but concrete enough to keep.
- **Over-humility / hedging:** none. The community candor paragraph is not over-humility — it's the strategy Eric prescribed, and it doubles as a culture signal for a company whose stated values include candor. Keep it.
- **Register match (02-behavioral-profile.md):** the letter's direct, systems-thinking voice ("instrument it, find the feedback loops that matter") is authentically his — that's the builder-communicator pattern. What's missing from his natural register is the mission-warmth thread his past letters always carry; the optional mission sentence above addresses it. His past letters also over-ran into effusiveness ("completely enraptured"), which this draft correctly avoids — don't add superlatives when adding warmth.
- **Sign-off:** "Kind regards" deviates from his documented "Sincerely," formula (fixed in edit 13).
- **Headline:** if the application has a subject line or PDF title, apply the style guide formula without the em-dash: "Senior Developer Relations Partner: AI solutions engineer specializing in agentic platform enablement."
