# Front Desk Staff — Touchstone Climbing (Berkeley Ironworks)

Source: https://ats.rippling.com/touchstone-climbing/jobs/bef8d523-5585-4d61-aa3d-bfba9c260ed5
Fetched: 2026-07-20 (posting live at fetch time)

## Company Overview
Touchstone Climbing operates 18 climbing gyms across California and positions itself as "more than gyms -- We're a community." The organization emphasizes competitive compensation, benefits, and gym membership perks for employees.

## Position Details
Part-time role at Berkeley Ironworks: "no more than 30 hours per week" and "more often under 20 hours per week." Front desk staff serve as the gym's primary community contact ("the heartbeat of our community"), welcoming members and ensuring smooth operations.

## Key Responsibilities
- Processing waivers, check-ins, and membership transactions
- Greeting visitors, answering calls, and responding to emails
- Managing calendar reservations
- Resolving customer service issues professionally
- Retail sales of merchandise and climbing gear
- Monitoring gym safety standards and providing guidance
- Maintaining facility cleanliness
- Supporting team culture

## Required Qualifications
- "Flexible availability, including evenings and weekends (no exceptions)"
- Climbing knowledge and safety awareness (climbing passion with knowledge of terminology and safety practices)
- Composure managing diverse customer situations
- Self-direction and initiative
- Computer and point-of-sale system proficiency
- Multitasking ability in fast-paced environments

## Preferred Qualifications
- Customer service, service industry, or office management background
- Current CPR and First Aid certification

## Compensation & Benefits
Starting pay: $21.00/hour (varies by facility location, membership volume, experience, tenure). Benefits include Medical FSA, paid time off, free gym membership across all facilities, and flexible scheduling.

## Location
Berkeley Ironworks, 800 Potter Street, Berkeley, CA 94710
